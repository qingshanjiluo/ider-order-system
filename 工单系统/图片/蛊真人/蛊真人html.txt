            </p>

            </p>

            </p>
        </div>
        
        <div class="vein-card reveal">
            <div class="vein-number">VII.</div>
            <h3 class="vein-name">瞳蛊脉</h3>
            <div class="vein-name-sub">Oculus Fames</div>
            <p class="vein-desc">

            </p>
        </div>
        
        <div class="vein-card reveal">
            <div class="vein-number">VIII.</div>
            <h3 class="vein-name">梦蛊脉</h3>

                若有人在梦中唤其真名，梦蛊便会顺着梦境爬入现实。
            </p>
        </div>
        
        <div class="vein-card reveal">
            <div class="vein-number">IX.</div>
            <h3 class="vein-name">命蛊脉</h3>
            <div class="vein-name-sub">Fatum Nex</div>
            <p class="vein-desc">

                转嫁给他人。然命数有定，多取一分，天道便记一笔，
                终至九雷轰顶，形神俱灭。
            </p>
        </div>
    </div>
